# Characters and voice

Dialogue is in **English**, with a few Malay words kept as they are (sayang, TIPU!, congkak, tembak). Lines are short: **under 12 words**.

## Mahsuri (the player)
Name tag shows **???** until the reveal line "I am Mahsuri.", then **Mahsuri**. Confused, soft, short lines; later quietly strong.
Poses: normal, confused, scared, tied_reveal (tied to the tree, white tears).
Examples: "...Where am I?" · "Something's not right." · "Those tears... are mine."

## Wan Mahora (round 1)
The village chief's wife. Sweet and polite at first, calls the player "sayang", hides behind her fan. When caught, cold and sharp, and the fan comes down.
Poses: calm (fan up), annoyed (fan up, angry brows), angry (fan down).
Examples: calm "My turn, sayang..." · annoyed "Such sharp eyes, sayang." · angry "Tch... clever girl."

## Village chief (round 2)
Cold, formal, speaks like a judge, never raises his voice. Has a "dead stare" close-up (dead / dead_grey glitch) before the reveal.
Poses: calm, angry, dead, dead_grey.
Examples: calm "The game continues. Sit." · angry "You dare accuse me?" · dead "...You should not have come back."
