# The legend (version this game follows)

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahsuri

- Mahsuri lives in Langkawi in the early 1800s. She marries **Wan Darus**, who leaves to fight the Siamese.
- A travelling poet (Deramang) stays in the village; Mahsuri is kind to him.
- **Wan Mahora**, the village chief's wife, is jealous of her and spreads a rumour that Mahsuri betrayed her husband (adultery).
- The **village chief** judges her without letting her speak. She is tied to a tree. Ordinary weapons fail; she is killed with her family keris.
- Her blood runs **white**, proof that she was innocent. She curses Langkawi for seven generations.
- After her death: the Siamese invade (Nov 1821); villagers burn their rice so the enemy can't take it (*Padang Beras Terbakar*, the Field of Burnt Rice); crops fail and families leave; the island suffers for seven generations.

## Rules for the game
- Mahsuri is always the wronged hero.
- The accusation must be clear: she is accused of **betraying her husband / adultery**.
- The execution is **never shown**. Only symbols: the tree, the keris, the white blood. No gore.
- Treat the legend with respect; it is real Malaysian heritage and her tomb is still visited today.
