# Visual and audio style

- 1920×1080, desktop browser, scales to fit.
- Night kampung house lit by warm lamplight, textured darkness at the edges. Round 2 is outside at night: torches, big tree, house with a glowing window.
- Always-on **light** VHS look: scanlines, a little grain, slight chromatic offset. It gets stronger in round 2 (darker, red tint, tracking glitches). White flash at the reveal.
- **Never tint the characters.** Noelle's original drawing colours must come through; no yellow/sepia filters on people. Darken backgrounds instead.
- Memory photos are cream, deckled-edge cards with a red date stamp (e.g. "14 MAR 1817"), shuffled in a pile; memory 1 on a wooden table with an aged keris, memory 2 on the sandy outdoor ground.
- **Text boxes:** gold pointed frames from assets/ui. Story box = full width at the bottom (click to continue). Gameplay box = small, on the left, board visible, auto-advances. Name tag sits on the top-left edge of the box. Typewriter text.
- **TIPU!** stamp: bright red rubber-stamp look (assets/ui/tipu_stamp.png), slams in with a shake.
- On-screen VCR text in a chunky mono font: "▶ PLAY", "◀◀ REW", "■ STOP".
- Title screen font for the glitching TIPU!: Creepster (SIL OFL).
- Sound: crickets → whispers → silence + heartbeat; tape whirr on rewind; a heavy thunk for the stamp. Sound only starts after the first click.
- Creepy, not gory. No blood except the symbolic white blood.
