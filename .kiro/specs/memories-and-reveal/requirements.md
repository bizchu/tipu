1. AFTER winning round 1, THE player SHALL see Memory 1 (4 cards on the wooden table with the aged keris): wedding, husband leaving, the travelling poet, the whispers.
2. AFTER winning round 2, THE player SHALL see Memory 2 (3 cards on the sand): the judgement, the tree, the white blood.
3. EACH card SHALL be animated, dated with a red stamp, and narrated by ??? in one line; click advances.
4. THE reveal SHALL zoom into the tree photo, glitch, flash white, and show tied Mahsuri centred in front of the tree with the text box already on screen.
5. THE curse SHALL show four photos popping in from top-left to bottom-right without covering Mahsuri's face, each with her narration; THEN every photo SHALL be tappable to open a large view with date, title and description; CONTINUE SHALL appear only after all four are seen.
6. THE dawn SHALL fade in warmly with "■ STOP" and "The sun is rising... I can finally rest.", then roll the credits ending on THE END held centre screen.
7. The execution SHALL never be shown; no gore.
